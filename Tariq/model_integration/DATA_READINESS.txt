Data needed
1. BTC and ETH reference prices on a complete UTC one-minute time grid.
   Preserve validity flags and publication/availability times. Do not replace the
   original reference prices with arbitrary exchange close prices.
2. BTC market features for 5m, 15m and 1h (18 columns total):
   price_displacement, price_move_retention, flow_pressure, flow_cvd_slope,
   liq_side_pressure and liq_activity_severity, each suffixed __5m/__15m/__1h.
   The direction builder accepts these already-calculated market measurements;
   it does not implement their underlying registry definitions.
3. Enough earlier history to reproduce the owner's feature initialization.
   Exponentially weighted statistics depend on their initial history. Magnitude
   also uses historical same-clock-slot activity and weekday/weekend grouping.
   A single current price row or a 15-minute slice is insufficient. Use the owner's
   reference history or agree and verify a warm-up policy before claiming parity.
4. Source schema/registry mapping and the owner's validity and release guards.
   In particular, direction permits the origin minute price if already published;
   magnitude's builder shifts prices by one minute. Preserve each implementation.
5. Prefer one real reference input/output from the owner at a known timestamp for
   comparing our feature preparation. Synthetic package examples do not prove that
   features from raw data are calculated correctly.

Target output contracts
- Direction: 30 engineered features -> p_up.
- Magnitude: 27 engineered features plus positive scale_bps -> net_magnitude_bps.
  scale_bps comes from the magnitude build_features function; do not hard-code the
  synthetic example's scale of 12 for real data.
- Formula: (2*p_up - 1) * net_magnitude_bps = predicted_signed_score_bps.
- Actual return, if checked later, must use BTC reference prices at t+1 and t+16.
  Keep these future evaluation prices out of the forecast inputs.
