# Magnitude experiment history

The [registry](../results/experiment_registry.csv) contains 63 magnitude entries after removing the separate timing research. Entries are historical summaries, not a count of fitted models.

The progression includes the frozen benchmark, refitted pair, random-forest blending, feature additions, bootstrap/leaf/seed changes, and unsuccessful alternate models and shorter windows. See [numerical results](../reports/magnitude_results.md). Compact historical receipts can refer to intentionally pruned files.
