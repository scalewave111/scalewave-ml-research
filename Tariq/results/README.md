The registry records evidence references, not a claim that every old artifact is replayable. Compact original receipts remain beside the research code.

In the registry, magnitude improvement is baseline minus candidate in log bps. Staged timing improvement is percentage of baseline MAE. Positive means lower error. See score_units and improvement_units. Multi-candidate historical summaries preserve their exact score dictionaries rather than inventing a single winner.
